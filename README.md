A one-line office joke built on the most relatable meeting sin of all: the "quick update" that refuses to be quick. It opens on a standup that has already overstayed its welcome, piles on the familiar props — slides, a poll, a follow-up meeting — and lands on the punchline that the whole meeting existed only to schedule another meeting about the meeting, the loop every office worker recognises instantly.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the office joke to `entry.txt` inside the directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (defaults to `/output`), with no trailing newline.
